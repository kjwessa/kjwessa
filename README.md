## Explore (almost) Everything (aka, the approach)

Discovery is everything to me. I ask questions. Then more questions. I have a bias for action (the next line of code, the next dopamine hit), but I *know* if I skip this step, at some point, I’ll lose focus on what I’m building and for who (and why it matters to them).

Chris Do has been excellent for learning about the importance of discovery. The book Shape Up by Basecamp (I think) also showed me the importance of that. And then more recently, the book on constraints by David Epstein. All of them, solid.

## The Stack I Like (aka, getting weekends back)

On the backend, Payload CMS. Been using it since their v3 beta, and it’s such a solid product and I love how I can build anything with it. The big mental unlock for me was the first plugin I built using Tanstack Forms and Usesend. Tons of screw-ups but then it just clicked. After that, I built plugins for site settings, SEO integration, redirects and some other pieces.

The longer I used Next JS on the frontend though, the more I was curious about splitting them. When I discovered Astro, that was the right fit, and it felt opt-in for everything vs. the Next JS opt-out of the “blackbox” of magic approach. Never looked back, and I’m looking forward to Tanstack support in the upcoming v4. I think I’m done with Next JS. Useful to know it, but I think Tanstack aligns more with my no “magic” approach. I want control, to see how things are working. Spend more time building, less time debugging. A weekend with family vs. pouring through docs for an obscure bug fix.

Posthog for observability and product context has helped a ton. No, I don’t work for them (but I’d like to some day). Turso for the read-heavy sites, Cloudflare on everything (feel like I haven’t even scratched the surface), and Turborepo is the glue that holds it all together (but I’m exploring NX for more granular control).
